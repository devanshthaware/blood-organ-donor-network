# VeinLink — Trust Layer Architecture & Academic Synthesis

## 1. Problem Statement Alignment

The Blood & Organ Donor Network problem statement explicitly establishes the requirement for a multi-dimensional **Trust Layer**:

```text
                 TRUST LAYER
                     │
       ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
       ▼             ▼             ▼
 Explainability   Fairness     Uncertainty
       │             │             │
       └─────────────┼─────────────┘
                     ▼
               Human Oversight
                     │
                     ▼
                Audit Engine
                     │
                     ▼
             Cryptographic Proof
                     │
                     ▼
                Blockchain
```

Blockchain is therefore the **integrity and provenance component** of the Trust Layer, not the entire Trust Layer itself.

## 2. The 6 Pillars of the VeinLink Trust Layer

### Pillar 1: Explainability (XAI)
- Every algorithmic recommendation generated by the matching and allocation engines produces a human-readable, deterministic explanation breakdown:
  - Clinical urgency objective contribution ($35\%$).
  - Waiting time equity contribution ($25\%$).
  - Logistical distance & cold ischemia buffer ($35\%$).
  - Data completeness confidence ($5\%$).
- Explanations are hashed (`explanationHash`) and linked to the cryptographic provenance record.

### Pillar 2: Fairness & Equity
- Waiting time normalization prevents newly registered patients from starving long-waitlisted candidates with equal urgency.
- Pareto dominance optimization ensures that no candidate is recommended if another candidate strictly dominates them across all clinical and logistical dimensions.

### Pillar 3: Uncertainty & Confidence Reporting
- ML matching models and CV label extractions expose an explicit `confidence` metric ($\in [0.0, 1.0]$).
- Low-confidence extractions ($< 65\%$) or compressed logistical buffers automatically route to `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, preventing automated false confidence.

### Pillar 4: Mandatory Human Oversight
- **Anti-Autonomous Allocation Invariant**: Algorithmic recommendations remain strictly advisory. Final organ allocation requires authenticated coordinator review.
- **Human Override Tracking**: When a coordinator selects Rank #2 over Rank #1, the system enforces mandatory recording of clinical/operational justifications.

### Pillar 5: Continuous Auditability
- All events, status changes, and human interventions are immutably logged to `auditLogs` and `domainEvents`.
- Distinct actor classification: `user`, `coordinator`, `system`, `system:n8n`, `admin`.

### Pillar 6: Cryptographic Integrity Proofs (Blockchain)
- Audit records are converted into deterministic SHA-256 hashes, linked in an unbroken sequential hash chain, and anchored periodically to an external blockchain ledger.
- Anyone can independently verify that an audit record has not been altered since the moment it was anchored.
